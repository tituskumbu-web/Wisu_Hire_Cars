Our Story

Wisu Hire Cars began with a simple idea: car hire should be straightforward, transparent and dependable. What started as a small local fleet has grown into a trusted regional provider serving hundreds of customers every year.

We specialise in short- and long-term vehicle hire for leisure, business travel, corporate fleets and temporary replacements. Every vehicle in our range is carefully selected, regularly serviced and prepared to a high standard before it leaves our yard.

  • ✓ Independently owned and operated
  • ✓ Focus on late-model, well-maintained vehicles
  • ✓ Local knowledge with professional processes
  • ✓ Strong emphasis on safety and customer care
